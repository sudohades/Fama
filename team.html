Our Story

Agriflow was born from recognizing a critical gap in Kenya's agricultural sector. Smallholder farmers—particularly maize cultivators who form the backbone of our nation's food security—struggle with fragmented access to timely agricultural information, market prices, and financial resources. Whether facing unpredictable rainfall patterns, emerging pest threats like Fall Armyworm, or information asymmetries that keep them economically vulnerable, farmers operate with limited knowledge. Our team recognized that bridging these information and resource gaps could transform not just individual livelihoods, but strengthen the resilience of entire farming communities. We founded Agriflow to deliver real-time localized weather updates, early pest and disease warnings, best-practice agronomic guidance, daily market prices from major trading centers, and direct links to financing solutions—all tailored for the realities of small-scale farming.

We believe that social entrepreneurship succeeds when it deeply understands local needs and integrates innovation with practical financial support. By combining digital tools with community-driven learning, Agriflow empowers farmers to make better decisions, improve yields, reduce losses, access better market prices, and build sustainable livelihoods. Every feature we build is informed by conversations with farmers in the field, insights from agricultural research, and our commitment to making technology work reliably even in areas with limited internet connectivity. Our mission is clear: equip Kenya's farmers with the information and resources they need to transform farming from a struggle for subsistence into a profitable, resilient enterprise.
